01 / Project Overview

Understanding the Flow of Traffic

Traffic congestion affects travel time, road efficiency, and the daily movement of people. Traditional traffic signal systems may not always respond effectively to changing road conditions.

TrafficAI explores the use of artificial intelligence to analyze traffic information, predict possible congestion, and support better traffic signal timing decisions.
